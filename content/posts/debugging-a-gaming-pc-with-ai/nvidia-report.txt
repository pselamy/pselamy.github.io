GPU no-signal / GPU lost in Minecraft and OCCT; improved after AMD chipset update (causation unconfirmed)

Please investigate or route this report to the display-driver/desktop GPU team. This is a family-owned custom PC with repeatable failures before the chipset update, now passing initial validation. We are not asserting a confirmed NVIDIA or AMD defect, nor ruling out intermittent hardware/power/PCIe problems.

REPRODUCTION BEFORE MITIGATION
Launch Minecraft Java Edition 26.3 from Minecraft Launcher with bundled Java 25.0.1. Observed settings: 260 FPS cap, render and simulation distance 32, VSync off. Play for several minutes. Physical display loses signal while fans/RGB remain on; the PC can remain accessible over SSH. Vanilla and modded Minecraft have both been used; the Oct 6 monitored failure was vanilla. NVIDIA overlay was disabled and the failure persisted. Other overlays/background programs, including Crosshair X, were not fully isolated. Safe Mode had remained visible for at least 15 minutes. Minecraft settings were not normalized to a minimal-load profile.

INTERVENTION AND RESULTS
AMD chipset package 8.08.12.551 installed October 6 around 22:24, reboot 22:27. Six installer components reported success:
GPIO Promontory 3.0.3.0 -> 3.0.5.0; PPM provisioning 8.0.0.38 -> 8.0.0.65; 3D V-Cache optimizer 1.0.0.9 -> 1.0.0.12; PSP 5.32.0.0 -> 5.46.0.0; SMBus 5.12.0.38 -> 5.12.0.44; GPIO 2.2.0.134 -> 2.2.0.137.
October 7: identical 15-minute OCCT test passed with motherboard/iGPU display (core peak 65 C, 294.78 W), then passed with monitor directly on GeForce (67 C, 292.77 W); OCCT reported no errors and completed the schedule. User confirmed physical picture remained visible for the direct-GeForce test.
October 7 15:44–16:24: 40-minute Minecraft observation with monitor directly on GeForce, 1,168 valid GPU samples, 37–60 C, 0–99% utilization, 3.90–246.99 W. No captured GPU-lost condition or relevant Windows error. User confirmed vanilla and modded versions all worked without crashes. Four Java starts were intentional version changes, not crashes; this was not uninterrupted gameplay. One approximately 13-second telemetry gap was an expected recorder reconnect at its 33-minute stream lifetime, not established GPU loss.
NVIDIA 617.14 remained installed and BIOS was not updated. Reboots and display reconnections are confounders. We have not rolled back the chipset update to reproduce again, tested a replacement PSU/GPU, performed a dedicated VRAM test, or proven a permanent fix. Core temperature alone cannot exclude hotspot, VRAM or connector/power issues.
